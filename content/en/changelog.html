What's new in the Ultimate Nonogram Solver. The web version continues the original Excel VBA version (up to ver1.011) and starts at version 2.

v2.3.0 2026-11-23

  • Added a dedicated page for each of the 12 languages (/en/, /ko/, /zh-CN/, …) so the app can be found in searches worldwide.
  • Added the How to use, How it works and FAQ pages and this changelog (English and Japanese).
  • The header now shows the version and links to these pages, highlighting the page you are on.
  • When you open a page in a language different from your browser's, a bar suggests the page in your language.
  • Added information about the developer, with links to contact us or hire us on Coconala.

v2.2.0 2026-11-22

  • New "Find solvable settings" in the Creator tab: searches for settings that turn a photo into a uniquely solvable puzzle and shows candidates.
  • New "Find a solvable QR" in the QR Code tab: picks a QR code that is uniquely solvable and verified to scan.
  • Added a smoothing option, and a warning for dither and outline modes.
  • Fixed garbled text when encoding non-ASCII characters (e.g. Japanese) into QR codes.
  • Renamed the app to "Ultimate Nonogram Solver" (最強イラストロジック自動解答).

v2.1.0 2026-11-21

  • New "Import from image (AI)": let ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or another AI read a photo of a magazine puzzle, then paste the result.
  • The left menu can be collapsed, sections fold, and the main action buttons stay visible.
  • In the Creator tab, dragging the divider now resizes the preview images, and the generated puzzle fits the available area.
  • Improved the layout on phones.

v2.0.0 2026-04-16

  • First release of the web version, porting the Excel VBA solver to JavaScript (pure logic, no guessing).
  • Step-by-step visualization, loading and saving .txt files, and saving as an image.
  • Creator tab (puzzles from photos) and QR Code tab (puzzles from QR codes).
  • 12 languages and sample puzzles.
