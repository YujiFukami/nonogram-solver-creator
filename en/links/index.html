Puzzle magazines and books

Nonograms are loved all over the world under many different names, and magazines and books are published in many countries. When you solve puzzles on paper, try this app's "Import from image (AI)" and automatic solver too.

Information as of November 2026. Please check availability and prices on each site. This app is not affiliated with any publisher or shop listed here.

Japan (Illust Logic / Oekaki Logic)

  • Logic Paradise (ロジックパラダイス) — Gakken, bimonthly
    A magazine dedicated to nonograms, from mini puzzles to giant ones with over 10,000 cells.
  • Logic Mate (ロジックメイト) — Magazine Magazine, bimonthly. Publisher's puzzle site: Puzzle Mate
  • Logic Oekaki Time (ロジックお絵かきタイム) — Tokyo Sanseisha, bimonthly
  • All puzzle magazines on Fujisan (Japanese magazine store)

United Kingdom (Nonogram / Hanjie)

  • Puzzler Nonogram / Giant Nonogram — Puzzler Media
    A dedicated nonogram magazine since 1999 (formerly Hanjie), 13 issues a year, plus the large-format Giant Nonogram.
  • Subscription (DC Thomson Shop)

Korea (Nemo Logic / 네모로직)

  • Nemonemo Logic (네모네모 로직) series — Jeu Media
    A puzzle book series published since 1996, with new volumes several times a year.

Russia (Japanese crossword / Японский кроссворд)

  • Сакура. Японские кроссворды (Sakura. Japanese crosswords) — Russian Post subscription page
  • Щука. Японские кроссворды (Shchuka. Japanese crosswords, monthly) — Russian Post subscription page

Poland (Malowane liczbami)

  • Rozrywka. Nie tylko sudoku
    A monthly puzzle magazine with about 150 puzzles per issue, including "malowane liczbami" (painted by numbers).

Worldwide (Pic-a-Pix)

  • Conceptis Puzzles
    The puzzle maker behind "Pic-a-Pix", supplying puzzles to magazines, books and apps worldwide.

Names around the world

Nonogram, Griddlers, Hanjie, Paint by Numbers, Picross (English); イラストロジック / お絵かきロジック (Japanese); 네모로직 (Korean); Японский кроссворд (Russian); Logimage (French); Crucipixel (Italian); Nonogramm (German); Nonograma (Spanish); Malowane liczbami (Polish); Kare Karalamaca (Turkish); 数织 (Chinese).
