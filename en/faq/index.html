Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. No sign-up or installation is needed — just open it in your browser. The AI used by "Import from image" (ChatGPT etc.) runs on your own account, so there is nothing to pay to this app.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes. On phones the left menu opens on top of the grid: tap "▶" to open it and tap outside to close it. Large puzzles are easier on a PC.

How large can a puzzle be?

Up to 300 × 300 cells. A typical magazine puzzle of around 50 × 45 is solved in seconds.

Some cells stayed undecided. Why?

Usually either a clue was mistyped (a missing number or a shifted line — check that the row and column totals are equal), or the puzzle does not have a unique solution. The solver uses pure logic and never guesses, so ambiguous parts stay undecided.

Typing magazine puzzles by hand is tedious.

Use "Import from image (AI)". Send a photo of the puzzle with the dedicated prompt to ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, and paste the answer back. Reading errors are caught by the automatic total check.

My puzzle from a photo is not solvable, or does not look like the photo.

Use "Find solvable settings": it searches for settings that give a unique solution and shows candidates to choose from. Dithering and outline modes rarely give unique solutions. Choose a photo with clear contrast, crop it to the subject, and start with 30–60 columns.

Can the QR code puzzle really be scanned after solving?

QR codes picked by "Find a solvable QR" are verified by actually decoding them. Still, please scan it with your phone before printing. A #number may be added to the end of the URL, but it opens the same page.

Are my puzzles or images uploaded anywhere?

No. Solving, image conversion and QR generation all happen in your browser. Sending an image to an AI in "Import from image" is something you do yourself.

What is the file format?

The .txt format is the same as the original Excel VBA version: the size on the first line, followed by the column clues and the row clues.
